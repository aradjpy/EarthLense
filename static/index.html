🧪For curious scientistsWant to see how we checked our answer?⌄

Our app fits a straight line to complete-year averages. These numbers help us check whether the line shows a convincing trend.

P-value—Smaller than 0.05 suggests a detectable trend under the model assumptions.
R²—How much variation the straight line describes.
95% interval for the trend—A plausible range for the slope, assuming the model fits.

This data comes from NASA gridded model/satellite-derived products, not a sensor on your street. A trend doesn't prove why a change happened. The statistical check assumes independent, similarly variable errors, which might not hold for time series.
